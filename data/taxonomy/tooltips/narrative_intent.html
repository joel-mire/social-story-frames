Some common reasons people tell a story in a social media post include:
  • express/demonstrate one’s identity or beliefs
  • teach a life lesson
  • justify or challenge a belief or social norm
  • entertain
  • release pent up emotions (vent)
  • convey need for emotional support
  • convey a similar experience
  • correct misunderstandings or add missing details associated with an event/situation
These are just a few ideas to help you think. Answer in your own words as precisely as possible. Don’t copy directly from this list unless it truly is the best answer.
