Some common overall goals for posting on social media include:
  • request or provide facts or info about approaches, strategies, etc
  • seek or provide emotional support.
  • express/reinforce one’s identity, values, or accomplishments.
  • solicit or share experiences to gain or share insights.
  • argue/advocate for a viewpoint to persuade others.
  • share an enjoyable or funny post.
